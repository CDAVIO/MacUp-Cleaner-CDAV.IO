The ghost in “System Data”

Hidden caches — not a broken Mac

macOS keeps caches, logs and crash reports in hidden folders. Over months they grow to many gigabytes and show up in Settings as “System Data”. Apps rebuild them by themselves, so clearing them is safe.

No need to format or reset your Mac

Erasing a Mac to win back space takes hours and you have to restore everything. Clearing caches takes a minute — and it all goes to the Trash first, so you can put it back.

An honest answer

Does a Mac need a paid antivirus?

For most people, no. Every Mac already includes free protection that Apple updates automatically. A yearly “virus cleaner” subscription doesn’t make it stronger.

Already built into your Mac — free

  • XProtectApple’s malware scanner, updated in the background
  • GatekeeperChecks apps before they open
  • System Integrity ProtectionLocks the core system files
  • FileVaultEncrypts your disk if the Mac is lost
  • FirewallBlocks unwanted incoming connections
  • Security responsesUrgent security fixes install by themselves
⚠︎ VIRUS DETECTED!Your Mac is infected (3)Clean now — $69/yr

“Your Mac is infected!” pop-ups are scams

Close the tab. Don’t call the number, don’t install what it offers, don’t pay.

What really keeps a Mac safe

  • Keep macOS updated
  • Install apps from the App Store or developers you trust
  • Keep FileVault and the firewall on — macup protect shows both

MacUp is not an antivirus. It shows whether the protection already in macOS is switched on.

Safety first

Trash only

Cleaning moves files to the Trash. MacUp never empties it and never deletes for good.

Questions

Is MacUp really free?

Yes. It’s open source under the MIT license — no trial, no subscription, no account, no ads.

Why Terminal and not an app?

A Terminal tool is small and transparent: no installer, no admin password, no background process. You can read every line it runs.

Is it safe to clear caches?

Yes. Caches are copies that apps rebuild by themselves. MacUp moves them to the Trash, so nothing is lost until you empty it, and files in use are skipped.

Will I lose passwords, bookmarks or documents?

No. MacUp never touches documents, photos, passwords or bookmarks. “Browser history only” is optional and off by default.

Why don’t I see my SSD’s life %?

macOS only reports S.M.A.R.T. Verified or Failing. Install the free smartmontools once and MacUp shows the life left %:

brew install smartmontools
Does it work on Intel Macs?

Yes — macOS 13 Ventura or newer, on Apple silicon and Intel.

How do I update or remove it?

macup update installs the latest version. macup uninstall removes it completely; your PDF reports stay in Downloads.

macup update
macup uninstall
Does MacUp send any data?

No. It works offline and has no telemetry. This website uses no cookies and no trackers.
