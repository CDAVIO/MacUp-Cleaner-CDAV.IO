Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

CDAV.IO publishes MacUp and the website macup.cdav.io. In short: we do not collect personal data — not through the tool and not through the Website.

1. The MacUp tool

  • MacUp runs entirely on your Mac. It reads system information (storage, SSD, battery, memory, processor, graphics, security settings) locally to show it to you.
  • It has no analytics, no telemetry and no account. Nothing about your Mac is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • PDF reports are saved in your Downloads folder and stay on your Mac. A local log of items moved to the Trash is kept at ~/.cache/apple-cleanup/operations.log so you can see what was moved.
  • MacUp connects to the internet only when you install or update it: it downloads files from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com and codeload.github.com). GitHub receives standard request data such as your IP address, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

2. The Website

  • No cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The Website loads no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • Your language and light/dark choice are saved only in your browser’s local storage, on your device. You can clear them in your browser settings at any time.
  • The Website is hosted by Netlify, Inc. Like any web host, Netlify processes technical request data (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) to deliver and protect the Website, under the Netlify Privacy Policy. We do not use this data to identify or profile visitors.

3. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you have a question or a request under the GDPR or another privacy law, contact us and we will answer it.

4. Children

MacUp and the Website are general-purpose tools and do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

5. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

6. Contact

Open an issue on GitHub or visit cdav.io. Security issues: please use private vulnerability reporting.
